How it works

Five steps, from the day you meet your egg to everyday care.

  1. 01Meet
  2. 02Name
  3. 03Incubate
  4. 04Hatch
  5. 05Care
  1. 01

    Meet

    Dr. Santoro shows you three eggs. You pick one.

    • Metro Egg
    • Corner Egg
    • Prism Egg
  2. 02

    Name

    You pick a Caller name, the one your Mon will know you by.

    H-Lynk Core
    Your line to your Mon.
  3. 03

    Incubate

    15 minutes, 30 minutes or an hour, then one notification when it's ready.

    • 15 minutes
    • 30 minutes
    • 1 hour
  4. 04

    Hatch

    Your Mon chooses too. The first look your Mon gives you is the yes.

    Be there when it opens.

  5. 05

    Care

    Feed, Rest and Play raise Fullness, Energy and Social.

    Your Mon asks. You answer.

    A look, a sound or a reach is how your Mon asks for a meal, a quiet night or a game.

    • Feed

      Your Mon: Asks for food.

      You: Share a meal. Every Mon has favorites.

      Fullness

    • Rest

      Your Mon: Gets sleepy.

      You: Lights down. Energy comes back while your Mon sleeps.

      Energy

    • Play

      Your Mon: Comes closer.

      You: Play a short game. Stop when your Mon steps back.

      Social

    A low meter is a request. Nothing is lost while you are away.

    Example levels, not a live reading.
