## Focus order

Intro: title → body → Start → Done-equivalent Back key. Playing: round label → twin buttons (Look left, Peek, Look right) → trackpad. Result: body → Social ring → Play again → Done.

## Timing (2.2.1) and gestures (2.5.1)

No time limit. Flick and tap have VoiceOver adjustable increment/decrement and activate, arrow keys and Enter on web/Quest, and the `TrackpadActions` buttons.

## Announcements

Round changes announce `m16.round.a11y`. Found / peek-out announce once each. Hiding motion is not announced; the focused spot label changes as the player looks.

## Reduced motion

Hide = fade out; found = fade in with a static happy pose; peek-out = fade in at the real spot; no bounce, no camera move.

## Sound

Found and peek-out sounds have text twins (announcements and the Mon's visible pose). Nothing depends on hearing.

## Dynamic Type

Twin button labels up to 12 characters; at XXL on SE they stack vertically under the scene and the scene scales down.
