# M02 Welcome: accessibility

Owner: `a11y-auditor`. Standard: WCAG 2.2 AA (https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), WAI-ARIA carousel pattern (https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/carousel/), Apple HIG accessibility, Android accessibility. Inputs: `03-direction.md`, `04-components.md`, `05-copy.md`, `06-critique.md`, `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `docs/COPY_DECK.md`, and the current kit source in `packages/ui` (read 2026-10-04).

**Verdict:** accessibility risks to fix. Two kit defects fail AA on the daylit page as the kit stands today, and the Skip button is under the touch minimum. All three are kit fixes, not screen workarounds.

## Kit defects found (block M02–M07)

1. **Ghost label on daylit.** `packages/ui/district/tones.ts` gives royal a `text` of `text-royal-300` for every scheme ("royal is too dark to read as text on night"). On `concrete-50` that is 2.07:1. The ghost label must resolve to the `accent` semantic token (royal-500 daylit 5.08, carolina night 7.89) or a light/dark pair. Affects Skip, Next, Sign in here and every ghost link on M03–M07.
2. **Error text on daylit.** `ErrorMessage` and `NEON_FIELD.message` use `text-apple-400`: 3.25:1 on `concrete-50`, 3.58:1 on white. They must use the `danger` token (apple-600 daylit 4.98, apple-400 night 5.68). Not on M02, but logged here because M02 is the first daylit screen in the run.
3. **Ghost `size="sm"` is 36 pt tall** (`min-h-9` in `Button.tsx`). Skip must be `size="md"` (44 pt, `min-h-11`) with `hitSlop` adding 2 pt per side on Android to reach 48 dp.

## Focus order

The carousel shows one panel at a time; panels not on screen are removed from the accessibility tree, so swiping never reads a hidden panel.

1. Skip (`m02.skip`)
2. Panel heading: `Heading level={1}` on panel 1 (`m02.p1.title`); `level={2}` on panels 2 and 3, inside the `Main` that panel 1's `h1` names
3. Panel body
4. Panel image (`Figure` with its alt)
5. Status row, only when offline (`m02.status.offline`)
6. Progress (`m02.progress.a11y`)
7. Previous arrow (absent on panel 1, not just disabled, so the first swipe after the image is Next)
8. Next arrow
9. Panels 1–2: Next button. Panel 3: Get started, then Sign in

On a page change, focus moves to the new panel's heading. Skip moves focus to panel 3's heading.

Duplicate control (low): panels 1–2 carry both a Next arrow and a full-width Next button with the same name and action. It passes 3.2.4 (consistent) but adds a swipe stop. Recommendation for `design-director`: keep the full-width button (bigger, in thumb reach) and hide the arrow Next from assistive tech on panels 1–2, or drop it.

## Dynamic Type

Default sizes fit SE (`05-copy.md` length budget). At larger sizes:

| Size | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Up to XXL | caption band grows; image shrinks from 62% toward 45% of height; actions stay pinned to the bottom 40% |
| Above XXL (accessibility sizes) | image floors at 35% of height; the caption band becomes a scroll view between image and actions; actions stay pinned and never scroll away |
| Panel 2 captions | at accessibility sizes the three captions move from under each egg into a stacked list below the image; "Bodega Baddiee Cee Bloodline" wraps, never truncates |
| Buttons | labels wrap to two lines instead of truncating (`whitespace-nowrap` in `Button.tsx` must be lifted for `size="lg"` full-width buttons) |

The `whitespace-nowrap` label class is a third kit change: at XXL "Already a Caller? Sign in" (25 characters) will clip on SE without it.

## Responsive and foldable

Material 3 width classes (compact < 600, medium 600–839, expanded 840–1199, extra-large ≥ 1200 dp).

| Class / posture | Layout |
|---|---|
| Compact | single column as drawn; 16 pt gutter |
| Medium | single column, content max 560 pt centred, 24 pt gutter |
| Expanded, extra-large | two panes: image left (55%), caption band, progress and actions right; one panel at a time |
| Book posture | image in the leading pane, text and actions in the trailing pane; the action group (Next or Get started + Sign in) stays whole in one pane |
| Tabletop posture | image above the hinge, caption and actions below |

Hinge rects come from `@acme/ui/adaptive-panes` (port in progress; `DEVICE_CHECKS.md`). Until it lands, medium and above use the single centred column. A centred column crosses a vertical hinge, so on a detected fold the column moves into the leading pane.
