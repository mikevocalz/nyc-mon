## Risks

- **Panel 2 conflicts with Decision #5.** The prompt's panel 2 shows "three starters in one frame, idle". Under Decision #5 the Caller meets three *eggs* at M08 and only sees the Baby after the hatch. Showing living starters here either spoils the hatch or sets up the expectation that you pick a creature, not an egg. If panel 2 shows Mons, which forms? The Babies (#002 Squeaklet, #009 Kittee Cee, #062 Yotito) give away the reveal; Mid forms (#004 Hood Ratti, #011 Bodega Cee, #064 Barrio-Yote) promise a stage Phase 1 never reaches (Law 7). This goes to Mike.
- **"Scanning isn't recruiting"** is canon (`V11 ¶43`: "Scanning is not recruitment") but scanning is out of scope in Phase 1. New players may not know what scanning is, so the line may read as a non-sequitur. Test comprehension.
- **Language (Law 9):** panel 3 introduces "Caller". It is the first time the player sees the word, and M07 depends on them understanding it. Never "owner", "trainer" or "user". "Callah" never appears here; it is Mon dialogue only. Free-living Mons are "Hood Mons", never "wild".
- **Culture without stereotype (§2.3):** a "real block" is a specific place. Choosing one block risks reading as "this is where these people live". Pair the block with no starter that would map a community to a species (`V11 ¶53`).
- **Skip must always be visible** (§4.1). Teens skip first and read later, if ever (assumption in `PERSONAS.md`).
- **No paywall at the end**: BUILD_PROMPT rejects Tolan's.
