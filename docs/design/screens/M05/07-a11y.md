# M05 Parental consent: accessibility

Owner: `a11y-auditor`. Standard: WCAG 2.2 AA (https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), Apple HIG and Android accessibility. Readers: a child under 13 on screen, a parent or guardian in the email. Inputs: `03-direction.md`, `04-components.md`, `05-copy.md`, `06-critique.md`, canon Decision #15, `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `docs/COPY_DECK.md`, kit source in `packages/ui`.

**Verdict:** accessibility risks to fix. The structure is kind and clear. Kit defects carry over (ghost links, error colour), the kit `Badge` text is below the 13 pt floor, and the denied screen must be readable in one pass by a child using a screen reader.

## Contrast

Method: `contrastRatio()` from `packages/theme/contrast.ts`, 2026-10-04, hexes from `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` (theme agent's in-flight `tokens.ts` matched at measurement time). Both schemes ship (D8). The denied screen stays daylit in light mode (`03-direction.md`); in OS dark mode it follows dark like every other M05 state, because overriding the OS appearance for one screen would be the jarring choice.

## Kit changes this screen needs

1. Shared: ghost label → `accent`; `ErrorMessage` → `danger`; `TextField surface="daylit"`; `Button variant="cta"`.
2. **`Badge` text size.** `Badge.tsx` sets `xs` labels at 10 px and `sm` at 12 px (`text-xs`). `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` sets `type-caption` 13 pt as the floor. The `neutral` tone variant ships at `size="md"` (14 px) or the `StatusRow` item renders its own `type-caption` text. Smaller badges stay legal only as decoration elsewhere.
3. `Badge tone="neutral"`: daylit `concrete-700` on `concrete-100`; night `silver` on `surface-sunken`. Both measured above.
4. `EmptyState illustration` slot: when the slot is empty (Santoro art is `TODO(canon)`), the layout must not leave a gap or an unlabelled image.

## Focus order

**Ask**

1. Back
2. Title `m05.ask.title`, `Heading level={1}`
3. Body
4. Guardian email field
5. Field hint (read as the field's hint, not a separate stop)
6. Preview toggle; when expanded, the email text follows it in order
7. Send

**Sent (replaces the form):** focus moves to `m05.sent.title`. Then body, Keep playing, Send it again, Change email. Change email returns to the form with focus in the field and the email kept.

**Denied / expired (full screen on next app open):** focus starts on the title. Order: title, body paragraphs in reading order (reason, Mon, data, later), illustration only once it exists, then the action(s). No other element precedes the title: the status row is not shown on this screen.

**Status row on later screens (M07, and M08+ outside the H-Lynk top band):** the pending badge is the first element after the screen's own back button, so a screen-reader user meets it before the screen title. Not focusable; read as text.

## Child-readable checks (beyond WCAG)

- Body copy is short sentences in common words (`COPY_DECK.md` § Age-appropriate). Reading order on the denied screen puts "you didn't do anything wrong" before the data sentence, so the reassurance comes first for a listener.
- No timeout, no countdown, no urgency on any state (also 2.2.1).
- The denied screen never shows deletion of the Mon (Law 8, Decision #15).

## Findings

| # | Issue | WCAG | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ghost links and errors fail on daylit (kit) | 1.4.3 | Blocker | shared kit fix |

| 3 | Approved toast could time out before a child hears it | 2.2.1 | High | stays until dismissed or read; polite |
| 4 | Empty illustration slot could leave an unlabelled image | 1.1.1 | Medium | render nothing until art and alt exist |
| 5 | Status row reads before the title on later screens | 1.3.2 | Low | intended: the status is context for the screen |
