# M04 Birth year gate: accessibility

Owner: `a11y-auditor`. Standard: WCAG 2.2 AA (https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), WAI-ARIA radio group pattern (https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/radio/), Apple HIG and Android accessibility. Neutrality constraints: FTC COPPA FAQ D.7, H.3 (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions). Inputs: `03-direction.md`, `04-components.md`, `05-copy.md`, `06-critique.md`, `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `docs/COPY_DECK.md`.

**Verdict:** likely accessible foundation once the shared kit fixes land. Replacing a wheel with buttons is the right call for VoiceOver and Switch Control users (`01-research.md`). The accessibility layer must stay as neutral as the visible copy: no hint, announcement or state may differ by the year chosen.

## Neutrality in the accessibility layer

The same rules that bind the visible copy (`05-copy.md`) bind every spoken string and state:

- No tile is selected, focused or highlighted on arrival. Initial focus goes to the title, never into the grid.
- No announcement fires on selecting a year except the selection itself (`m04.year.a11y.selected`). Nothing is announced on Continue that differs by year.
- The Continue hint (`m04.continue.a11y.hint`) names the year back and nothing else.
- No haptic difference between years.

## Focus order

Step 1 (decades):

1. Back (`m04.back.a11y.label`)
2. Title, `Heading level={1}`
3. Why line
4. Decades group (`m04.grid.decades.a11y.label`), tiles in reading order, left to right, top to bottom, oldest first
5. Type it instead (`m04.type_instead`)

Step 2 (years): after a decade is chosen, focus moves to the years group label (`m04.grid.years.a11y.label`), then the year tiles, Change decade, Type it instead, then Continue once a year is selected. Change decade returns focus to the decades group label with nothing selected.

Typed: Type it instead swaps the grid for the field and moves focus into it; Pick from a list instead swaps back and focuses the decades group.

Selecting a year only reveals Continue below the grid. That is not a change of context (3.2.2); focus stays on the selected tile. Continue appears in the order after the grid links.

| Birth year field | text field | `m04.field.label` | `m04.field.hint`; `keyboardType="number-pad"`, `maxLength={4}`, `textContentType="none"`, `autoComplete="off"` (no autofill of a birthday) |

Decade labels: VoiceOver reads "1990s" as "nineteen-nineties"; TalkBack may read "one thousand nine hundred ninety s". Set an explicit `accessibilityLabel` per tile from a spoken form ("1990s" → "the 1990s") only if TalkBack testing shows the raw read is wrong; the visible label stays `m04.decade.label`. Logged as a device check.

## Keyboard (web)

| Element | Tab | Enter / Space | Escape | Arrows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Decades group | one Tab stop per tile (they are buttons) | opens that decade | — | Arrow keys move between tiles as a convenience grid |
| Years radiogroup | one Tab stop (roving `tabindex`; none checked → first tile) | Space checks | — | Arrows move and check (APG radio pattern) |
| Field | Tab | Enter = Continue | — | — |
| Continue | Tab | activate | — | — |

Roving focus on arrival at step 2 lands on the first year tile **without checking it**, so the keyboard path does not create a default (FAQ D.7). This deviates from the APG note that arrowing checks; checking happens only on Space or Arrow movement after the user acts.

## Findings

| # | Issue | WCAG | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ghost links 2.07:1 and 36 pt in the kit today | 1.4.3, target size | Blocker | shared kit fix |
| 2 | Validation 3.25:1 | 1.4.3 | Blocker | `danger` token |
| 3 | Selected year must not rely on the royal edge alone | 1.4.1 | High | check glyph + `checked` state (specified) |
| 4 | Keyboard radiogroup must not preselect on focus | 3.2.1 and FAQ D.7 | High | roving focus without checking |
| 5 | Birthday autofill could fill a year the child did not choose | FAQ D.7 | Medium | `autoComplete="off"` on the field |
| 6 | Decade read-out on TalkBack | 1.1.1 | Low | verify on device; spoken label if needed |
