## Structure and focus order

`header` (nav, skip link to `#content`) → `main`: hero `section` (h1, paragraph, CTA link, starters `ul`, district radio group) → H-Lynk `section` (h2, `figure`) → starters `section` (h2, three `article` cards with h3) → care `section` (h2, three items with h3 + meter) → hatch `section` (h2) → `footer`. One h1; no skipped levels. Dividers are `aria-hidden`.

## Labels

- City background: "{District} street grid, seen from above".
- Seal: `BrandLogo` alt from the kit ("NYC-MON").
- DeviceStage: `figure aria-label="H-Lynk device"` + visible `figcaption`. Canvas itself decorative.
- Meters: each `ProgressBar` has a visible label ("Fullness 70%"). "Example meter levels" sits under them.
- Starters list: `aria-label="The three starters"`.
