## Focus order (VoiceOver / TalkBack / keyboard on web and Quest)

1. Status chip (LED text) · 2. Mon name and bloodline · 3. Rings group (each ring read as progressbar) · 4. The Mon (button, "say hi") · 5. Feed · 6. Rest/Wake · 7. Play · 8. Dex · 9. Home key (disabled here) · 10. Menu · 11. Trackpad · 12. Back · 13. Forward (disabled).

## Dynamic Type

Name and status wrap to two lines at XXL; ring captions scale to `type-label` and the rings shrink from `md` to `sm` before any caption truncates. Care bar labels stay one line on SE at XXL (8 characters max in copy); above that, the bar becomes two rows of two. Test on SE (§8 asks XXL without clipping on M13).

## Other

- Haptics: trackpad tap light; ask answered success (via `packages/ui/haptics`). Haptics off in settings disables them; nothing depends on them.
- Sound: the Mon's ask sound has a visual twin (the chip), so muted players lose nothing.
- Quest: every control shows a hover state; focus ring visible on ray hover; no target under 48 dp.
