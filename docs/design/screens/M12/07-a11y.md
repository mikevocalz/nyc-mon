# M12 Hatch: accessibility

Owner: `a11y-auditor`. Standard: WCAG 2.2 AA, Apple HIG, Android accessibility, W3C XR Accessibility User Requirements (https://www.w3.org/TR/xaur/) for the Quest 2D window. Inputs: `03-direction.md`, `04-components.md`, `05-copy.md`, `screens/M11/07-a11y.md`.

**Verdict:** accessible by design. The moment is fully followable by announcement, fully completable without holding, gesture or timing, and its single light event stays inside the flash threshold.

## Contrast

Method: `contrastRatio()` (`packages/theme/contrast.ts`), 2026-10-08. The hatch is always night (Decision #4).

## Focus order

**pre:** status light ("Status light: Ready to hatch") → case image (`m11.case.a11y.label.ready`) → trackpad "Open the case" → its labelled button → keys.

**in progress:** focus moves to the screen container once, which reads the current phase line; then skip (when shown) → trackpad (inert except hesitate) → keys. Focus is never moved again during the show: announcements carry the phases so focus doesn't jump under the Caller's finger.

**hesitate:** trackpad "Stay close" (hint: "Holds you close. Double-tap and hold, or use the Stay close button.") → labelled button "Stay close" (single activation resolves it; no hold needed).

**complete:** plate (`m12.plate.a11y.label`) → CTA → keys. Focus lands on the plate when complete is reached.

## Timing (WCAG 2.2.1, 2.2.2)

- Nothing on M12 requires a response within a time. Skip is optional; complete waits forever; hesitate waits forever (the 8 s hint is help, not a timeout).
- The show runs about 8 s (4 s reduced) and is controllable by skip: it does not need a pause control under 2.2.2 (under 5 s of auto-play is exempt; above that, skip ends it).

## Flash (WCAG 2.3.1)

One luminance rise and fall in `burst`, peak `orange-300` at ≤ 60% opacity (≤ 40% on Quest), no saturated red flash (the scan line is 40% opacity and moves, it does not flash). One flash in any second. Reduced motion removes the burst entirely.

## Reduced motion

Authored sequence in `03-direction.md` "Reduced motion"; read from the sim-core adapter (§3.6). No camera drift in either mode on a 2D panel; in 3D the continuity dolly becomes an instant reframe.

## Haptics

Never the only channel: every haptic beat has an announcement or a visible change. Respect M19's haptics setting. Quest 2D: no haptics reach the controller from the phone API; nothing is lost because nothing depended on them.

## Screen reader and the 2D art

The Baby plate's label comes from `CREATURE_ART[].alt` (already pronoun-free per PS-005) until models arrive; with models, the label is `{babyName}, {bloodlineLabel}` plus the current performance ("looking at you"), never a description of a painting that is no longer shown.

## Dynamic Type

The plate steps down from `type-station` to `type-title` (M07 contract). At AX sizes the CTA wraps to two lines inside `TrackpadActions`; the skip pill grows to the left and never overlaps the scanner head.

## Quest 2D window

Same centred shell as M11. Pinch-and-hold drives "Stay close"; ray hover shows focus on skip and the trackpad. Keep the burst cap at 40%: the panel at arm's length fills more of the field than a phone does, and headset users can't glance away from a bright panel as easily (XAUR: avoid sudden luminance changes).
