# M03 Sign in / Create account: accessibility

Owner: `a11y-auditor`. Standard: WCAG 2.2 AA (https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), including 3.3.7 Redundant Entry and 3.3.8 Accessible Authentication (Minimum). Apple HIG Sign in with Apple (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sign-in-with-apple), Google branding (https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines). Inputs: `03-direction.md`, `04-components.md`, `05-copy.md`, `06-critique.md`, `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `docs/COPY_DECK.md`, kit source in `packages/ui`.

**Verdict:** accessibility risks to fix. The design is sound (passkey and social sign-in satisfy 3.3.8 without a cognitive test; per-provider text errors). The kit's error colour and ghost links fail on daylit, and inline legal links need an underline.

## Kit changes this screen needs

1. Ghost label → `accent` (shared with M02).
2. **`TextField` daylit look.** Today every field is a night well with an orange nameplate (`cards/neon-field.ts`). It passes contrast but the direction specifies a white face with a `concrete-600` edge on daylit. Add `surface?: 'well' | 'daylit'` (literal union; default `'well'` keeps existing screens), with the label above the field in `type-label` `text` colour.
3. `ErrorMessage` and `NEON_FIELD.message` → `danger` token.
4. The error glyph in `ErrorMessage` (a 10 pt apple square, `aria-hidden`) is decorative; the text carries the error, so colour is not the only cue (1.4.1). Keep it hidden from AT.

## Focus order

1. Back (`m03.back.a11y.label`)
2. Title, `Heading level={1}` (`m03.title.create` / `.sign_in`)
3. Subtitle
4. Intent switch (`m03.switch.to_create` / `.to_sign_in`)
5. Apple, Google, Passkey (if supported), Email (in that order; hidden passkey leaves no gap)
6. Email expanded: Email field, Password field, Show password, Submit, Forgot password (sign-in only)
7. Error for the provider that failed, read in place directly after that provider's button
8. Legal notice (create only), then Terms, Privacy Policy, Children's privacy

Focus rules:

- Tapping "Continue with email" expands in place and moves focus to the Email field.
- On an error, focus moves to the error text (assertive announcement) only for errors after submit; client validation while typing announces politely and leaves focus in the field.
- When verification is sent, the email block is replaced and focus moves to `m03.verify.title`.
- When a system sheet (Apple, Google, passkey) closes, focus returns to the button that opened it. A cancel shows nothing and announces nothing (`05-copy.md`).
- Loading: the tapped button stays focused and reports busy; the other providers become disabled but stay in the order.

## Accessible authentication (3.3.8)

Apple, Google and passkey need no memorised secret or transcription: they pass by mechanism. The email path asks for a password, which passes only because paste and password managers work: `textContentType` must be set and paste must never be blocked. No CAPTCHA on this screen. If the server adds bot protection later, it must be a non-cognitive check.

Redundant entry (3.3.7): "Change email" from the verify block and "That email already has an account. Sign in instead" both keep the typed email (`05-copy.md`).

## Dynamic Type

Default: the page fits SE above y ≈ 600 pt (`03-direction.md`). At XXL the page scrolls inside `KeyboardAwareScroll`; legal links stay reachable at the end. Provider buttons grow in height to fit their label; labels wrap rather than truncate (`whitespace-nowrap` kit change from M02). The native Apple button keeps its own text sizing; give it a height of at least 48 pt and let it scale per Apple's guidance.

## Findings

| # | Issue | WCAG | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Error text 3.25:1 on daylit | 1.4.3 | Blocker | `danger` token in the kit |
| 2 | Ghost links 2.07:1 and 36 pt | 1.4.3, target size | Blocker | `accent` label, `size="md"` |
| 3 | Inline "Terms" and "Privacy Policy" in the legal notice differ from text by colour only | 1.4.1 | High | Underline links inside running text (`Link` with `underline`) |
| 4 | Errors must sit with the provider that failed and be announced | 3.3.1, 4.1.3 | High | Per-provider `ErrorMessage`, `role="alert"` after submit |
| 5 | Throttle countdown could chatter | 4.1.3 | Medium | Update the accessible label at most every 10 s |
| 6 | Password paste and managers must work | 3.3.8 | High | `textContentType`, never block paste |
| 7 | Field look contradicts the daylit direction | consistency | Medium | `TextField surface="daylit"` |
