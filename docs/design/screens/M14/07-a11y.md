Standard WCAG 2.2 AA. Ratios measured 2026-10-08 (formula of `packages/theme/contrast.ts`).

## Focus order

Sheet opens with focus on the first tile (not on Close), because feeding is the job. Order: tiles left to right → Close → shell keys → trackpad. Focus is trapped inside the sheet and the shell; Escape (web/Quest) and Back close the sheet and return focus to `m13-bar-feed`.

## Alternatives to drag (2.5.7)

Tap a tile, double-tap with VoiceOver/TalkBack, Enter on web, or trackpad flick + tap. All feed the same food through the same `applyCare` call.

## Announcements

`m14.eating.a11y` when the clip starts, `m14.eaten.a11y` when the ring settles. The overfed and declined lines are announced once. No announcement per ring frame.

## Reduced motion

| Moment | Reduced sibling |
|---|---|
| Tray rise | fade 200 ms |
| Tile lift | colour flash |
| Drop into the Mon | fade out |
| Eat clip | authored reduced clip (fewer bites, no head bob) |
| Feed particles | absent |
| Turn-away (full) / refuse | static turned pose |

## Dynamic Type

Tiles grow to 120 pt; names wrap to two lines then truncate with the full name in the label. At XXL on SE the tray takes 60% of the screen and the Mon scales down; the Mon's head stays visible. Full/declined text wraps; never truncated.

## Quest

Hover lifts the tile 2 dp and draws the focus edge. Ray drag works; pinch-select feeds directly (same as tap).
