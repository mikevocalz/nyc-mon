# NYC-MON — Eggs, Babies & Complete Form Roster

**Roster reconciliation v11.1 — 4 October 2026**

## Corrected count

- **Current reconciled roster: 115 records across 19 evolutionary families.**
- The older **109 / 18-family** count is the v8 baseline, not the current working total.
- **#001–#109** remain the preserved baseline.
- The post-v8 **Rentgoose family** is appended as **#110–#115** instead of reusing #062–#066, because those IDs already belong to the Yote family.
- The Rentgoose concept art never established an egg name, so **#110 is a reserved Egg record with its name still OPEN** rather than inventing a fake canon name.
- The old **37-Max-endpoint** count must not be repeated as current until F19 staging is fully reconciled.

## Lifecycle rule

Current canon is **Egg → Baby → Small → Mid → Max**. A hatched Mon is the Baby form and remains Baby until an authored evolution event.

F19 was drawn before that lifecycle was fully reconciled and contains five post-egg goose designs. This file preserves every design while flagging **Lordy Hordie** for stage mapping rather than silently inventing an extra universal life stage.

## Post-v8 reconciliation notes

### Rentgoose addition

The October 3 Rentgoose art introduced a new goose family after the 109-record roster. Several concept sheets accidentally labeled its forms #062–#066; that range is already occupied by Yotito, Lil’ Yote, Barrio-Yote, Fuego-Yote and Yote del Eléctrico. The family therefore continues the registry at #110.

The latest recovered design naming is preserved here as **Squabblet → Ren Mc Duckie → Leasebeak → Lordy Hordie → Grand Gander**. Earlier goose-sheet names such as **Squaby Goose / Tenant Goose / Property Goose / Landlord Goose / Rentgoose** are retained as draft-history names, not duplicate Dex forms.

**Rebbe Goose** and **Sleazy Goose Landlord** remain concept-art identities unless/until they receive explicit independent evolution families. They are not double-counted here.

### Not additional records

LanternFye, Cockie Roy, the Nuru butterfly line, Vanto Yin, Vanta Yang, Hawk Fu Young and the Yote family were already inside #001–#109. Their newer voice, culture, rigging and animation work does not create extra Dex records by itself.

Mechanics examples such as **Subway Ratti, Central Park Stag, Hydrant Hound, Boiler Beast** and the **EngineX Sewer Prototype** are not silently numbered here. They need an explicit promotion to roster canon before consuming a permanent Dex ID.

## Remaining roster decision

F19 still needs one creator decision: the official **egg name** and the v11 lifecycle placement of **Lordy Hordie** relative to **Grand Gander**. Until that decision, the roster count remains **115 records**, but the Max-endpoint subtotal is intentionally not frozen in this document.
